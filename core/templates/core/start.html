The problem it solves

Every two weeks, someone collects time clock exports from each store. Every store's file looks different. Some punches are missing, some have typos, people clock in at the wrong store, and overtime has to be worked out across weeks. Today that means hours of checking line by line in a spreadsheet, and mistakes still get through to people's pay.

How it works, in 4 steps

  1. 1

    Files come in as they are

    Each store sends its export in its own format. Nobody has to clean it up first.

    See the raw files →
  2. 2

    Every row gets checked

    The pay rules are applied: rates per store, breaks, overtime. Simple, safe fixes are handled by rule ({{ rule_count }} in this period) and written down so you can see them.

    See what was fixed by rule →
  3. 3

    A person decides the unsure parts

    Only {{ review_count }} items go to the review queue. Each one says what looks wrong, why, and what the likely fix is. You approve with one click or enter the right value.

    Open the review queue →
  4. 4

    Payroll is ready to run

    One line per employee, with every amount explained step by step. Nothing is final until a person signs off. The checked numbers then go to the payroll provider.

What the words on the screens mean

Gross
Total pay earned before anything is taken off: regular hours, overtime and extras.
Net before taxes
Gross minus deductions like loan repayments. Taxes are worked out later by the payroll provider.
Blocking
Something is missing or broken, so payroll can't be finished until it's fixed.
Needs review
The system has a likely answer but isn't sure, so a person decides.
Handled by rule
A known, safe fix was applied automatically and logged.
Attendance to discuss
Lateness or absences worth a conversation. They never block payroll.
